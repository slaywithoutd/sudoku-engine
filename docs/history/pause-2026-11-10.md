## 4. Review findings on the M3 draft, not yet applied

Summary of the independent review of 2026-11-10 (read-only, nothing executed). Line numbers refer to the WIP commit `44128e3`.

1. **Per-count reservation.** A count reserves about 198,301 work units (M2.6 baseline, T02 row), not "some tens of thousands". The cost table in §4.6 and the whole-run budget rule ("time limit × 120") therefore do not hold: 100–200 counts cost 19.8M–39.7M units in reservations alone, against 21.6M at 180 s.
2. **Stale certificates.** Stage-2 "necessary" certificates can go stale once helpers are added. Every mark must be re-validated against the helpers, or downgraded to `not-tested`, before the first Perfect snapshot, and A04 must check certificate validity.
3. **Wrong outcome.** The §6 target counts only `proposal`; it should also count `already-satisfied`.
4. **Duplicate inputs.** A02's 132 inputs include the empty grid three times, so only 130 are distinct (10 sparse, not 12).
5. **Benchmark figures.** "Count verified ms" excludes worker start-up. The "30–60 ms" figure is not in the records, and "7.1–7.5k units per step" is recorded for the easy puzzle only.
6. **Duplicate givens.** They do not fail assembly. A conflicting grid assembles and the exact root exhausts; the `!assembled.ok → zero` branch is unreachable for a normalized classic grid.
7. **A06.** It does not contain the workspace check that §7 says it has.
8. **`AssistResult` shape.** It cannot express everything described: no `not-applicable` outcome, no `impossible` reason, no "at least one" count, no step or open-cell counts, and an undefined proposal payload.
9. **Unique evidence.** The evidence for Unique is the generator's `exhausted` event, not a `CountProof`, which only `runSolver` builds.
10. **Product choices outside §8.** The format-version bump, the aesthetics non-goal and the out-of-scope list need §8 entries.
